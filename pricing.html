How does the free trial work?
Sign up and your 7-day trial starts right away, with every feature on. No credit card is needed to start. At the end of the trial, subscribe to keep going.
Is there a contract?
No. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time from the portal or by email. Your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.
I have more than one location.
Pricing is per location. Email us and we'll help you set up each venue.
Do I need Square?
No. Inventory, alerts, ordering and the rest work by text with any POS. Connecting Square adds sales-based forecasts and weekly reports. Toast, Clover and Lightspeed are in development.

They are not part of the product yet, so they are not part of what you pay for today. We mark them so you can see where Sidecar is going.
Can I get my data out?
Yes. Export inventory, task history and message logs as CSV from the portal whenever you like.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026 · Sidecar HQ LLC · New York, NY

1. What We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Sidecar:

  • Account information: bar name, contact name, phone number, email address, neighborhood, social media handles
  • Business information: distributor names and emails, inventory data, brand voice, POS connection details
  • Usage data: SMS messages sent and received, tasks created, content generated, feature usage
  • Billing information: processed securely by Stripe — we do not store credit card numbers

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Sidecar service
  • To generate content on your behalf (social posts, order drafts, review responses)
  • To send you SMS notifications, alerts, and communications related to your account
  • To process billing and manage your subscription
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or policy violations
  • To contact you about product updates or service changes (you may opt out at any time)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Twilio (SMS delivery), Stripe (billing), Supabase (database), Anthropic (AI processing), OpenAI (image generation), Airtable (task management), Resend (email delivery)
  • Distributors: Only the order information you explicitly ask us to send on your behalf
  • Law enforcement: If required by applicable law or valid legal process

All third-party providers are contractually required to handle your data in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

4. SMS Data and TCPA Compliance

By using Sidecar via SMS, you consent to receive text messages. We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and CTIA guidelines. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. We maintain records of consent for all SMS communications. Message and data rates may apply.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after termination to allow for dispute resolution. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing ryan@sidecarhq.cc. Some data may be retained longer if required by law.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), access controls, and secure third-party infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your account credentials and notify us immediately of any suspected unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data portability (export your data from the portal)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ryan@sidecarhq.cc.

8. Cookies

Our marketing website (sidecarhq.cc) uses minimal cookies for analytics. The portal (portal.sidecarhq.cc) uses session cookies required for authentication. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Sidecar is not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active customers of material changes by SMS or email. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email ryan@sidecarhq.cc or write to Sidecar HQ LLC, New York, NY.

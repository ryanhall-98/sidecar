Independent hospitality deserves better tools.

Bars and restaurants are some of the hardest businesses to run well, on some of the thinnest margins. The software built for them mostly adds another login. We want to take work away.

Why Sidecar exists

An owner today runs a POS, a spreadsheet for counts, a stack of distributor emails, an accounting tool and a phone full of texts. Each one knows a piece of the business. The owner is the only one who sees all of it.

Sidecar is the layer that sees all of it too, and then does something about it: what to order, what changed, what needs a decision today.

How we build

  • Outcomes over features.

    Owners don't care what powers a system. They care about fewer stockouts, less waste, better prices and their evenings back. That is what we measure ourselves on.

  • Meet people where they work.

    The person running a bar is on the floor, not at a desk. That's why Sidecar starts with a text message and keeps the portal for when you want the full picture.

  • Owners stay in control.

    Sidecar prepares the work and you approve it. Nothing goes out on your behalf without your OK.

  • Say what's real.

    Every feature on this site is marked available now or in development. We would rather show you an honest roadmap than oversell.
